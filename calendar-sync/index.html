About Calendar Sync

Calendar Sync is a Google Workspace Add-on that helps users who work across multiple Google accounts keep their availability consistent. It reads events from secondary calendars and creates private "Busy [domain]" blocks on your primary calendar automatically — without copying titles, descriptions, attendees, or any other event details.

Privacy-preserving

Only the time slot is copied. Titles, descriptions, locations, and attendees are never shared.

Automatic sync

An hourly trigger keeps calendars in sync with no manual action required.

Up to 10 calendars

Connect your work, personal, and client calendars in one place.

Incremental sync

Uses Google Calendar sync tokens so only changed events are processed each run.

Setup Guide

What you need

  • A Google account
  • Google Calendar open in a browser
  • The Calendar Sync add-on installed

Step 1 — Install the add-on

Open the Google Workspace Marketplace and search for Calendar Sync. Click Install and follow the prompts. Grant the requested permissions when asked. Open Google Calendar — a Calendar Sync panel appears in the right sidebar.

Step 2 — Add your calendars

In the Calendar Sync panel, click Settings. Calendar 1 is your primary calendar — enter your main Google account email (e.g. you@work.com). Add your secondary calendars below (e.g. you@gmail.com, you@client.com). You can add up to 10 calendars.

Step 3 — Set your sync window

The sync window controls how far back and forward to create busy blocks.

  • Days in the past — how many days back to mirror (default: 7)
  • Days in the future — how many days ahead to mirror (default: 7)

Step 4 — Save and start

Click Save & Start Sync. The add-on will run a full sync immediately, create "Busy [domain]" blocks on your primary calendar, and set up an hourly trigger to keep everything in sync automatically.

What the busy blocks look like

Event on secondary calendarWhat appears on your primary calendar
"Team standup" (work)Busy [yourcompany.com]
"Doctor appointment" (personal)Busy [gmail.com]
All-day eventAll-day "Busy" block
Cancelled eventMirror removed
Rescheduled eventMirror updated

No titles, descriptions, locations, or attendees are ever copied — only the time slot.

Managing the sync

ActionWhat it does
PauseStops the hourly trigger (mirrors stay in place)
ResumeRestarts the hourly trigger
Full ResyncDeletes all mirrors and rebuilds from scratch
Stop & Clean UpDeletes all mirrors and removes the trigger

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Overview

Calendar Sync is a free Google Workspace Add-on that helps users synchronize busy time across multiple Google Calendar accounts. The add-on creates generic "Busy" calendar blocks so that availability from one configured calendar can be reflected in another calendar without copying the details of the original event.

Calendar Sync's use of Google user data complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Information We Access

Calendar Sync accesses Google Calendar data only as required to provide its synchronization functionality.

  • Calendar event timing information (start and end times) to determine when the user is busy.
  • Permission to create, update, and remove the "Busy" calendar events that it creates.
  • Your Google account email address to identify the calendar account being configured.

Calendar Sync does not use or store the titles, descriptions, attendee information, conference information, or locations of your existing calendar events.

How We Use Your Information

Information obtained from Google Calendar is used solely to provide the calendar synchronization functionality requested by the user. Calendar information is used to determine busy periods and to create or remove corresponding "Busy" blocks in configured calendars.

Your information is not used for advertising, profiling, marketing, analytics, artificial intelligence or machine-learning training, or any unrelated purpose.

Data Storage

Calendar Sync does not operate an external application server or database. Configuration information required by the add-on is stored using Google Apps Script User Properties and is associated with the individual Google account using the add-on. Calendar event information is processed within Google's infrastructure and is not stored in an external database controlled by the developer. Calendar Sync does not maintain copies of users' calendar event content outside Google services.

Data Sharing and Disclosure

  • Calendar Sync does not sell user data.
  • Calendar Sync does not share Google user data with advertisers, data brokers, analytics providers, or other third parties.
  • Calendar Sync does not transfer Google Calendar information to external servers or third-party services as part of its normal operation.
  • Human access to users' Google Calendar information is not provided as part of the service.

Third-Party Services

Calendar Sync does not use third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or data-processing services. The add-on operates using Google Workspace, Google Calendar APIs, and Google Apps Script services.

Data Retention and Deletion

Configuration data is retained only as necessary to provide the Calendar Sync functionality.

  • Users can remove calendar blocks created by Calendar Sync by opening the add-on and selecting "Remove all Busy blocks."
  • Users can clear or change their stored configuration through the "Reconfigure" option.
  • Users may also revoke Calendar Sync's access to their Google account through their Google Account security settings.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Calendar Sync's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the user-facing calendar synchronization functionality of Calendar Sync.

Security

Calendar Sync runs within Google Apps Script and Google's infrastructure. The add-on does not transmit Google Calendar information to an external developer-operated server. Access to Google Calendar data is controlled through Google's OAuth authorization system and the permissions granted by the user.
